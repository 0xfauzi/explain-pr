One skill and the evidence for it, as two aligned tracks with one cell per answer.

- The top track is the person's answers: filled `call` for a match, a `miss` cell with a stroke for a miss, a dashed cell for don't know.
- The track under it is what the easy answer would have scored on the same questions: filled `ink-3` or empty. Never give it a color.
- A column where the person matched and the easy answer did not is a catch. Mark it with a small `call` triangle above the column. Catches are what move a level.
- Dates run under the tracks, one label per day.
- Two figures at the same size sit to the right: the person's count and the easy answer's count.
- The level is a label at the right of the title: its name in `call`, then what earned it.
- Give the track `data-play` to fill its cells in order the first time the row is seen.
- Never show a percentage, a grade or a streak.
