Settings: every control as a ledger row, with the confirm step open on one of them.

- Three sections, each a `fh-sec` label and a ledger: How much, This project, Your record.
- The margin column names the setting. The control sits in the body. Nothing is boxed.
- A change that asks or sends more does not apply at once. The row it belongs to is replaced by `fh-confirm`, which states the change as one fact and waits for Confirm or Cancel. The screenshot shows it under the dial for a daily limit going from 1 to 2.
- Settings is current in the end group of the tab line. No tab is current.
