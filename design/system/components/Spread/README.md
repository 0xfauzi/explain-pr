The call beside the run, on one spread. Use it for every result.

- "Your answer" is always the left side and "The run" the right. Never swap them.
- The person's answer is `fh-answer`: italic serif in `call`. Under it, in `fact`, the names it refers to.
- Each side is headed by its time: when the call was locked and when the run finished.
- The run's facts are a `fh-facts` list with leaders. Mark the fact the verdict turns on with `is-key`.
- The verdict mark sits on the perforation.
- Follow the spread with one `fh-finding` row: the verdict in words in the margin, then one sentence of what the run showed. A miss adds one sentence of what would have shown it.
- Do not put a button inside a spread.
