One ruled line that says a standing state or a problem, and the confirm step. None of them is a box, a banner or a color.

- `fh-notice` sits directly under the tabs and closes with a 1.5px `ink` rule. The margin column names the state in a `label`; the sentence is `ui`; one quiet action may sit at the right.
- Use it for Off, Quiet and a lost connection. One notice at a time: a lost connection replaces the others.
- `fh-err` is an error or refusal inside the thing it is about: a 1.5px `ink` rule, a `label` and the engine's sentence in `fact`. It is `ink`, never `miss`: `miss` belongs to Did not match alone.
- `fh-confirm` is the step before a change that asks or sends more. It replaces the control that was changed, between two `ink` rules: the engine's sentence in `voice`, the change as one fact with a leader, then Confirm and Cancel.
- A standing notice is `role="status"`. A lost connection and an error are `role="alert"`.
- Nothing here animates in or out.
