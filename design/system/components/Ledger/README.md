The record as numbered rows between rules. Use it for the journal and the inbox.

- A ledger opens with the double rule (`fh-ledger--head`).
- The margin column holds the entry number in `ink` and the time under it. Below 760px they move above the row on one line.
- The title is the task in the person's words, in `voice-md`. The verdict sits at the right.
- Then two tagged lines: YOU, their answer in blue italic; RUN, the fact in mono.
- Rows are never boxed and never have a background.
