The two motions that carry meaning, with a Replay button. Use this as the reference when building either.

- The result prints in a fixed order: perforation, verdict mark, the run's lines with their leaders, then the finding. Your call never moves, because it was already there.
- The highlighter passes behind the question once when a sheet appears.
- Give a spread `data-play` to play its sequence, and each fact row its index as `--i`.
- All durations and easings are tokens: `dur-line`, `dur-stamp`, `dur-stroke`, `stagger`, `ease-out`, `ease-stamp`, `ease-pen`.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion`, everything is in its final state at once.
